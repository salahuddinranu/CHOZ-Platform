Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

CHOZ ("we", "our", "us") operates the CHOZ platform at this website. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Encrypted password (handled by Supabase Auth, we never see plain passwords)
  • Profile information you provide (if any)

Activity Data

  • Comparisons you create and complete
  • Decision Memories you save
  • Favorites and saved items
  • Feedback you submit

Technical Data

  • Basic session information required for authentication
  • Anonymized usage events for the platform analytics

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To show your comparison history, memories, and favorites
  • To improve the platform
  • To send transactional emails (email verification, password reset, security alerts)

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your comparisons with other users.
  • We do not send promotional emails without your consent.
  • We do not diagnose personality or mental health from your activity.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides encrypted storage and Row Level Security (RLS) — only you can access your own private data.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage for your theme preference (dark/light mode) and session management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your data in your Dashboard.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account at any time from Settings.
  • Correction: You can update your profile information.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and database
  • Cloudflare — hosting and CDN
  • Google — optional Google sign-in (if you choose to use it)

8. Children

CHOZ is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page.
