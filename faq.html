Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how CHOZ works, privacy, and accounts.

What is CHOZ?

CHOZ is a blind decision and comparison platform. You compare options without seeing brand names, so you choose based on what actually matters to you.

How does blind comparison work?

When you start a comparison, CHOZ shows you anonymous options labeled A, B, and C. Brand names stay hidden until you lock your choice and reveal the result.

Why do brands stay hidden?

Brand names, logos, and popularity influence decisions before people evaluate what they actually need. Hiding them helps you focus on what matters to you.

How is the result calculated?

CHOZ compares attributes based on the priorities you set. The result is based on stored data and your priorities — never on advertising or affiliate commissions.

Is CHOZ free?

Yes, CHOZ is free to use. You can create an account, compare options, and save decision memories without paying.

How is my data handled?

See the Privacy Policy. Your comparisons, memories, and favorites are private to you.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to Dashboard → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and associated data.

What is Decision Memory?

Decision Memory lets you save what you chose, why, and when. You can revisit past decisions any time.

Is CHOZ a shopping site?

No. CHOZ is a decision platform, not a store. We help you decide — you buy wherever you choose.

How do I report a problem?

Use the Contact page. We review all reports.

Do you use my data to train AI?

No. Your private comparisons and memories are not used for AI training.

Can I suggest new categories?

Yes! Use the Contact page and choose "Feedback" — we review all suggestions.
