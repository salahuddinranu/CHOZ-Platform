How CHOZ Works — A Complete Guide

📅 January 2026 · ⏱️ 5 min read · By CHOZ Team

CHOZ is built on a simple idea: brand names shouldn't decide your purchases. You should.

Here's how the platform works, step by step.

The Problem with Traditional Comparison

Most comparison sites are paid by brands. Reviews are sponsored. Rankings are influenced by commission rates. And even when they're honest, we as buyers still see logos and names — which triggers bias before we evaluate facts.

CHOZ's Approach: Blind Comparison

We remove brand names during comparison. You see anonymous options — Option A, B, C — with their specs. Only after you lock your choice do we reveal the brand.

The CHOZ Flow

  1. Choose a category — Phones, bikes, hotels, laptops, and 60+ more.
  2. See anonymous options — No brand names, no logos, no bias.
  3. Set your priorities — Battery? Price? Comfort? You decide the order.
  4. Lock your choice — Commit to one option.
  5. Reveal — The brand name appears.
  6. Understand — "Why I Chose This" explains the fit based on your priorities.
  7. Save to Memory — Store your decision for future reference.
  8. Share — Generate a clean share card.

Why This Works

  • No bias: You evaluate facts, not logos.
  • Your priorities drive the result: Not marketing, not ads.
  • Transparent: Every result shows the reasoning.
  • No manipulation: We don't push a specific brand.

What CHOZ Doesn't Do

  • We don't sell products.
  • We don't take payments to rank brands higher.
  • We don't fabricate reviews or specs.
  • We don't decide for you — we help you decide.
