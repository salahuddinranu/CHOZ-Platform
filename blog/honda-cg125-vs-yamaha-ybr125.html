Honda CG 125 vs Yamaha YBR 125 — Complete Comparison

📅 January 2026 · ⏱️ 6 min read · By CHOZ Team

These are the two most popular 125cc bikes in Pakistan. But which one is right for you? Let's compare without bias.

Engine & Performance

Honda CG 125: 124cc single-cylinder, 4-stroke. Known for reliability and torque. Top speed ~100 km/h.

Yamaha YBR 125: 125cc air-cooled engine. Slightly refined and smoother at higher RPMs. Top speed ~105 km/h.

Mileage

Honda CG 125: ~45 km/l (real-world average)

Yamaha YBR 125: ~50 km/l with careful riding

Winner: Yamaha YBR 125 by a small margin.

Comfort

Honda CG 125: Classic upright position. Firm seat. Good for short commutes.

Yamaha YBR 125: Softer suspension, better seat cushioning. More comfortable for longer rides.

Winner: Yamaha YBR 125.

Maintenance & Parts

Honda CG 125: Parts available everywhere. Cheap to maintain. Every mechanic knows this bike.

Yamaha YBR 125: Parts slightly more expensive. Fewer mechanics but still widely available in cities.

Winner: Honda CG 125.

Price (2026)

Honda CG 125: ~234,900 PKR

Yamaha YBR 125: ~299,000 PKR

Winner: Honda CG 125 (by ~65,000 PKR).

Verdict

  • Choose Honda CG 125 if: You want reliability, cheap maintenance, and lower upfront cost.
  • Choose Yamaha YBR 125 if: You want comfort, better mileage, and don't mind the higher price.
