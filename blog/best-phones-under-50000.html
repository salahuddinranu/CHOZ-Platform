Best Phones Under 50,000 PKR in Pakistan (2026)

Looking for a phone under 50,000 PKR doesn't mean settling for less. In 2026, the Pakistani smartphone market offers impressive options that handle everything from photography to gaming.

But here's the problem: most buying guides are paid promotions. This guide takes a different approach — we compare phones based on what actually matters to you.

What to Look For

Before diving into specific phones, understand what makes a good budget phone:

  • Battery: 5000 mAh is the new standard. Anything less is a compromise.
  • Processor: Snapdragon 6-series or MediaTek Dimensity 7000-series for smooth performance.
  • Camera: 50 MP is common now, but software optimization matters more than megapixels.
  • Display: AMOLED is better than LCD for colors and battery.
  • Storage: 128GB minimum with UFS 2.2 for faster loading.

Top Picks Under 50,000 PKR

1. Xiaomi Redmi Note 13

The Redmi Note 13 hits the sweet spot between price and performance. With a 5000 mAh battery, 33W fast charging, and a 108 MP main camera, it's a solid all-rounder for around 45,000 PKR.

2. Samsung Galaxy A15

Samsung's budget offering brings AMOLED display and 4 years of software updates. If long-term software support matters, this is worth considering.

3. Infinix Note 40

For those prioritizing battery life, the Infinix Note 40 offers 45W fast charging and a large 6.78" display. Great for media consumption.

4. Realme C67

A reliable choice with good build quality and a clean software experience. Battery and daily performance are strong.

How to Choose — Match Your Priorities

The "best" phone depends on what you value:

  • If battery matters most: 5000 mAh+ and 33W+ charging
  • If camera matters most: OIS and sensor size, not megapixels
  • If performance matters most: Snapdragon 7-series or Dimensity 8000-series
  • If longevity matters most: Check manufacturer's software update policy
