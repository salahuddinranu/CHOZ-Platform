Best Laptops for Students in Pakistan Under 100,000

📅 January 2026 · ⏱️ 6 min read · By CHOZ Team

Starting university? Buying your first laptop? You don't need the most expensive machine — you need the right one. Here's what actually matters for student life.

What Students Actually Need

  • Battery life: 8+ hours. Charging points in classrooms are rare.
  • Weight: Under 1.8 kg. You'll carry it everywhere.
  • RAM: 8 GB minimum, 16 GB ideal. 8 GB gets tight with Chrome tabs.
  • Storage: 512 GB SSD. 256 GB fills up fast with projects and media.
  • Display: 14-15.6 inches, full HD minimum, IPS panel.
  • Keyboard: Full-size with good travel for long typing sessions.

Top Picks Under 100,000 PKR

1. Acer Aspire 5

Reliable, upgradeable, and affordable. Great for general studies, browsing, and documents.

2. Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3

Balanced performance and portability. Ideal for students who move between classes.

3. HP Pavilion 15

Solid build quality, good keyboard, and dependable performance for coursework.

4. Asus Vivobook 15

Lightweight design with a bright display. Good for media and general use.

What to Skip

  • Chromebooks (limited software support in Pakistan)
  • Heavy gaming laptops (3+ kg is painful for daily use)
  • 4 GB RAM models (they slow down quickly)
