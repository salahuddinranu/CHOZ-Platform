Best Bikes Under 200,000 PKR in Pakistan (2026)

📅 January 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read · By CHOZ Team

Not everyone wants to spend 300,000+ on a bike. If your budget is under 200,000 PKR, you still have solid options. Here's a practical guide.

What to Prioritize

  • Mileage: Aim for 45+ km/l for daily commuting.
  • Engine reliability: Stick to known brands with local parts availability.
  • Comfort: Seat quality and suspension matter for long rides.
  • Resale value: Popular brands hold value better.

Top Options Under 200,000

1. Road Prince RP 70

Around 120,000 PKR. Great for students and short commutes. Simple, cheap, reliable.

2. Super Power SP 125

Around 175,000 PKR. Good value, decent performance for the price.

3. United US 125

Around 185,000 PKR. Widely available parts, cheap maintenance.

4. Used Honda CG 125

If you can find a well-maintained used one, it can be under 200,000. Best value in the long run.

What to Avoid

  • Unknown Chinese brands with no local support
  • Bikes with aftermarket engine swaps
  • Overpriced used bikes with hidden damage

Final Thoughts
