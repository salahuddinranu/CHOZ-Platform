About CHOZ

A blind decision platform built on a simple belief: you should choose based on what matters to you — not on brand names, logos, or marketing noise.

Web owned and created by Salahuddin Ranu.

Our Mission

CHOZ exists to remove noise from decisions. When you compare products, services, or options, brand influence often crowds out your actual priorities. We hide brands until you have made your choice — based on what you truly care about.

How It Works

You pick a category, view anonymous options, set your priorities, and lock your choice. Only then do we reveal the brand. Afterwards, CHOZ shows you a transparent "Why I Chose" explanation based on the data you saw.

What We Believe

Transparency

Every result is based on stored data. No fabricated explanations. No hidden favorites.

User Priority First

Our comparison engine is driven by your priorities, not by advertiser payments or affiliate commissions.

No Manipulation

We never push a specific product. The choice is always yours.

Privacy by Design

Your comparisons, memories, and decisions remain yours. Delete them anytime.
